About Us

Our story

Gotti Farms is a family farm in Gudipudi, Sattenapalli Mandal, Palnadu District, Andhra Pradesh. We have been farming for more than 20 years, and today we grow rice, turmeric, groundnut and seasonal vegetables on 12 acres, together with our partner farmers.

We started selling directly to families because we wanted people to get food the way we eat it at home: fresh, clean and naturally grown, without middlemen.

How we grow

  • Naturally grown: we use farmyard manure, crop rotation and natural pest control wherever we can. We are not organic-certified, so we do not call our produce "organic".
  • Traditional processing: our turmeric is boiled, sun-dried and ground in small batches; our rice is cleaned and dried on the farm.
  • Fair to farmers: partner farmers are paid fairly and on time.

Who we are

Gotti Farms is run by Madamanchi Bhanu Prakash in Gudipudi village. After more than 20 years of farming, we started selling directly to families so that the care that goes into our fields reaches your kitchen, with no middlemen in between.
