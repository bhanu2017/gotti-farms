Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver to your doorstep. If you are not sure whether we deliver to your PIN code, message us on WhatsApp (+91 83091 83156) and we will confirm.

Delivery time

Orders are usually delivered in 2 to 5 days, depending on your location after they are confirmed. Fresh vegetables are harvested and packed close to the delivery date, so delivery times can change with weather and harvest.

Delivery charges

  • Free delivery on orders above ₹499.
  • A delivery charge of ₹40 applies to smaller orders. It is shown before you place the order.

Order tracking

You can see the status of your order any time under My Orders. We may also call or message you before delivery.

If you are not at home

Our delivery partner will call you. If we cannot reach you after 2 attempts, we will contact you to arrange another time.

Contact
