Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information Gotti Farms ("we") collects when you use gugulefarms.com and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, email (optional), delivery address and the items you order.
  • Offer sign-ups: the WhatsApp number or email you share to get a discount code.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by our payment partner (Razorpay). We do not see or store your card or UPI details.
  • Your device: your cart, saved products, language and recent orders are stored in your own browser. If analytics is switched on, Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited.

How we use it

  • To deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To send offers and harvest updates if you signed up. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To improve our website and products.

Sharing

We share your name, phone number and address only with the people who deliver your order, and payment information only with our payment partner. We do not sell your information.
