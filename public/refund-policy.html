Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Fresh produce

Because vegetables and other fresh produce spoil quickly, we cannot accept returns of fresh items. If anything arrives damaged, spoiled or wrong, tell us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo, and we will replace it or refund it.

Rice, turmeric, groundnut, seeds and other packed items

If a packed item is damaged, the seal is broken or you received the wrong item, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. Please keep the item and packaging so we can check it.

How refunds are paid

  • Online payments (UPI / cards): refunded to the same account or card within 5–7 working days after we approve the refund.
  • Cash on Delivery: refunded by UPI or bank transfer to an account you share with us.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge before the order is packed. Call or WhatsApp us with your order number. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.
